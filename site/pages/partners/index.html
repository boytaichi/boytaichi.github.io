What you can already do with PSRE today

  • Hold it in the app of the brand that gave it to you; nothing expires.
  • Claim control of your balance by naming a wallet address of your own: from then on, only you decide what happens to it. If the brand recorded your wallet address when it created your balance, you can make that claim yourself, directly on the chain. If the brand's app manages your balance with no address recorded, the brand claims it for you, naming the address you give it.
  • Withdraw PSRE from that balance to your wallet whenever you choose. Claiming and withdrawing are two separate actions; you can claim today and withdraw next year, or never.
  • Trade it on the open market: a PSRE/USDC pool is live on Uniswap v3 on Base (linked on the Token page).

Want your brand on this page?

A partner is any business that would rather give customers a reward they keep than a discount they forget. Joining takes one PartnerVault and an entry deposit at the protocol minimum. Read how it works below.

Two vaults, one number.

The vault model in plain words. A PartnerVault is a brand's account with the protocol: a contract the brand creates once, funds, and uses to hold PSRE and hand it out as rewards. A CustomerVault is a customer's balance inside that brand's world: a small contract the brand's backend creates the first time it rewards that customer. The customer can claim control of it at any moment by naming a wallet address; that is one action. Who may make that claim depends on how the balance was created: a customer whose wallet address was recorded as the intended customer at creation may claim directly; a balance the brand's app manages, created with no intended customer (address(0)), can only be claimed by the brand's PartnerVault owner, on the customer's behalf and to the address the customer names. Withdrawing PSRE from it to that wallet is a second, separate action, taken whenever the customer chooses. The brand pays the gas for both vaults; the customer never needs a wallet to use the app.

The protocol watches one number per brand: PSRE held in the PartnerVault plus PSRE held in all of its CustomerVaults, counted only through flows the contracts themselves track. That number has an all-time high. Every week, if the number beats its high, the protocol releases new PSRE to the brand; if it does not, nothing happens and the high stands. PSRE that leaves those contracts, whether a customer withdraws it to a personal wallet or the brand transfers it out, lowers the number but never the high. Reward PSRE the protocol itself released is excluded from the next reading, so rewards cannot feed on themselves.

What a brand commits to. An entry deposit at the protocol minimum, which earns nothing and sets the baseline; gas for its own vaults; and its own reward rules, which the protocol does not set. The protocol commits to a fixed cap of 21 million PSRE, a 12.6 million reserve that opens only through verified growth, a 48-hour timelock on the four queued reward-parameter groups (base rate; epoch ceiling; partner share; and the tier group of thresholds and multipliers), and a 7-day timelock on contract upgrades. Other settings — the partner cap, the fee-tier whitelist, the entry minimum under the PSRE-native version, the staker-pool split, and pausing — are set by the governance multisig without a timelock. Partner SDK — the flow your backend runs

Written against the first real consumer: a backend that settles rewards weekly, converts the owed amount into PSRE in one tracked step per batch, and credits each recipient's CustomerVault. Calls tagged USDC-live are the contracts deployed on 22 April 2026 (factory 0xFF84…6902); calls tagged PSRE-native are the post-launch source, staged and not yet on mainnet. The BlockApex audit (2–17 April 2026, 29 findings, all resolved) covers the deployed code at commit 31eb313; the PSRE-native source has not had a BlockApex review. Untagged steps are identical under both. Decimals: PSRE 18, USDC 6.

  1. Register — the owner key calls the factory once. USDC-live approve USDC to the factory (≥ 500 USDC), then createVault(usdcAmountIn, minPsreOut, deadline, 10000); the PSRE received sets initialCumS. PSRE-native approve PSRE to the factory (≥ psreMin()), then createVault(psreAmountIn). Record the vault address from VaultCreated; one vault per creator address; at most maxPartners() active vaults.
  2. Fund — approval targets differ: the factory for creation, the PartnerVault for every later buy. Never pre-transfer USDC or PSRE to the vault: a raw transfer does not feed buy() and raw PSRE is ignored by ecosystemBalance.
  3. Buy — one tracked buy per batch. USDC-live buy(amountIn, minAmountOut, deadline, 10000) → psreOut; fix every credit in the batch at creditPSRE = owedUSDC × psreOut ÷ usdcAmountIn in integer arithmetic, residue kept in the vault. minAmountOut is your own floor from a fresh Quoter read; pass fee 10000 only (the only live pool). PSRE-native acquire PSRE by any route, then buy(psreAmountIn).
  4. Create on first credit — deployCustomerVault(partnerVault, customer) with customer = the recipient's wallet if known, else address(0) (platform-managed). Store recipient id → CustomerVault; verify factory.isCustomerVaultOf(cv). At most 1,000 registered CustomerVaults per PartnerVault; deregisterCustomerVault() frees a slot only for an empty vault.
  5. Credit — one distributeToCustomer(cv, amount) per credit; there is no batch form. Persist each logical credit with its nonce, its encrypted signed raw transaction and the hash before broadcast, so a crash can rebroadcast it. Internal move: ecosystemBalance unchanged.
  6. Read — show the customer the CustomerVault balance from the chain; show yourself ecosystemBalance, cumS, initialCumS, qualified, owedPartner, the current epoch, the last finalized epoch and the backlog.
  7. Claim by the holder, then withdraw — two separate actions. Claim: claimVault(newCustomer) by the intended customer (if set) or by the owner; one-time, irreversible, moves no PSRE, and takes the vault out of the owner key's reach (reclaimFromCV works only before claim). Withdraw: the claimed customer calls withdraw(amount) later; present it as its own step.
  8. Claim the protocol reward — after a paying epoch is finalized, the owner key calls RewardEngine.claimPartnerReward(vault); the PSRE arrives in the owner wallet, not the vault. PSRE-native bring it back in with buy(psreAmountIn); USDC-live there is no tracked re-entry path yet. Rewards never expire.
  9. Leakage — three flows lower ecosystemBalance: a claimed customer's withdraw, your transferOut, and anything that moves PSRE into the StakingVault. Claim is not leakage. cumS never falls. Partners never call reportLeakage.

Reads your app needs

  • Factory: getAllVaults() filtered by vaultActive(vault) for the live list; activeVaultCount() for the headline count; vaultCount() only when labeled "all-time". vaultOf/partnerOf are creation-time mappings — authorization reads PartnerVault.owner().
  • PartnerVault: ecosystemBalance(), cumS(), initialCumS(), lastEpochCumS(), psreBalance(), owner(), pendingOwner(), getCustomerVaultCount(), isQualified().
  • CustomerVault: psreBalance(), customer(), customerClaimed(), intendedCustomer(), parentVault(); cross-check with PSRE.balanceOf(cv).
  • RewardEngine: owedPartner(vault), totalClaimed(vault), qualified(vault), R(vault) and sumR() (tier is R/sumR against silverThreshold() 0.5% and goldThreshold() 2.0% — no tier getter), currentEpochId(), lastFinalizedEpoch(), paused(), pendingUpgrade(), upgradeTimestamp(). RewardEngine.vaultActive(vault) is the reward-activity flag, a different thing from the factory's decommission flag.

Errors your backend must handle

  • USDC-live output below your floor reverts at the router — retry with a fresh quote, never by loosening the floor; both floor parameters must be > 0; a past deadline reverts; createVault rejects fee tiers outside {100, 500, 3000, 10000}, buy forwards any tier to the router where a tier with no pool fails.
  • Epoch backlog and gas: autoFinalizeEpochs() runs inside createVault() and buy() and finalizes at most 10 pending epochs per call. Set the gas limit from eth_estimateGas on every call, never a constant.
  • Paused: RewardEngine.paused() stops createVault, buy, autoFinalizeEpochs and claimPartnerReward; PSRE.paused() stops every transfer. Read both before each batch.
  • deployCustomerVault reverts if the caller is not the vault's current owner or the vault holds 1,000 CustomerVaults; distributeToCustomer reverts on an unregistered vault, zero amount or insufficient vault balance (not on a claimed vault); claimVault reverts if already claimed, zero address, or unauthorized caller; reclaimFromCV reverts after claim; transferOut reverts toward any registered vault.
  • Owner rotation: updateOwner(newOwner) then acceptOwnership() from the new key; every onlyOwner call fails from the old key the moment it succeeds. A lost owner key is permanent: it strands the PartnerVault's funds and the address(0)-created CustomerVaults; vaults with a nonzero intended customer stay claimable by that address. Use a non-exportable HSM/KMS key with documented availability and recovery controls.
  • Partial batch failure: each credit is its own transaction; replay from your own logical ledger reconciled against receipts, never from events alone — DistributedToCustomer carries no batch id, recipient id or nonce.

Reading through the Prospereum RPC proxy

// The site's read-only proxy forwards eth_call / eth_blockNumber / eth_chainId / eth_getBlockByNumber
// to Base and allowlists the protocol contracts; it is not a write path. Public fallbacks: mainnet.base.org.
const rpc = async (method, params) => {
  const res = await fetch("https://prospereum.io/rpc", { method: "POST",
    headers: { "content-type": "application/json" },
    body: JSON.stringify({ jsonrpc: "2.0", id: 1, method, params }) });
  const j = await res.json(); if (j.error) throw new Error(j.error.message); return j.result;
};
const block = await rpc("eth_blockNumber", []);                       // pin every read to one block
const active = BigInt(await rpc("eth_call", [{ to: FACTORY, data: "0xda5ef232" /* activeVaultCount() */ }, block]));

Weekly batch: buy, then credit (one call per recipient)

const vaultAbi = parseAbi([
  "function buy(uint256 amountIn, uint256 minAmountOut, uint256 deadline, uint24 fee) returns (uint256 psreOut)",
  "function distributeToCustomer(address customerVault, uint256 amount)",
  "event PartnerBought(address indexed vault, uint256 amountIn, uint256 psreOut, uint256 ecosystemBalance, uint256 cumS)"]);
await wallet.writeContract({ address: USDC, abi: erc20, functionName: "approve", args: [VAULT, batchUsdc] });   // approve the VAULT this time
const buyTx = await wallet.writeContract({ address: VAULT, abi: vaultAbi, functionName: "buy", args: [batchUsdc, minOut, deadline, 10000] });
// 1. record buyTx + PartnerBought(usdcAmountIn, psreOut) before computing any credit
// 2. creditPSRE = owedUSDC * psreOut / usdcAmountIn  (BigInt floor; residue stays in the vault)
// 3. per credit: persist row → assign nonce → sign → persist signed raw tx + hash → broadcast → reconcile by receipt
for (const c of credits) {
  await wallet.writeContract({ address: VAULT, abi: vaultAbi, functionName: "distributeToCustomer", args: [c.customerVault, c.creditPsre], nonce: c.nonce });
}

Claim, then withdraw (customer side)

const cvAbi = parseAbi(["function claimVault(address newCustomer)", "function withdraw(uint256 amount)",

// intendedCustomer != 0: that address (or the owner) may claim; intendedCustomer == 0: only the PartnerVault owner.
await customerWallet.writeContract({ address: CV, abi: cvAbi, functionName: "claimVault", args: [customerWallet.account.address] });

await customerWallet.writeContract({ address: CV, abi: cvAbi, functionName: "withdraw", args: [amount] });   // this is leakage

Protocol reward

const engineAbi = parseAbi(["function owedPartner(address vault) view returns (uint256)", "function claimPartnerReward(address vault)"]);
const owed = await publicClient.readContract({ address: REWARD_ENGINE, abi: engineAbi, functionName: "owedPartner", args: [VAULT] });
if (owed > 0n) await wallet.writeContract({ address: REWARD_ENGINE, abi: engineAbi, functionName: "claimPartnerReward", args: [VAULT] });
// pays the OWNER wallet, not the vault; never describe it to customers as owed or expected — a flat week pays nothing

Rules a partner must not break

Hold PSRE nowhere except the PartnerVault and registered CustomerVaults; credit PSRE nowhere else; one PartnerVault per partner; never move a claimed holder's balance; never stake PSRE from a vault and count it as ecosystem balance; never describe the protocol reward to customers as owed or expected; never tell a customer that claiming moves PSRE anywhere — withdrawing does; never hold the owner key outside a non-exportable HSM/KMS, since the protocol has no recovery for a lost key.

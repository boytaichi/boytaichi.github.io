Governance

Governance and upgrades: a 2-of-3 multisig (Gnosis Safe) that owns the RewardEngine, the factory and the StakingVault. Treasury and liquidity: a 2-of-3 multisig.

Four queued parameter groups — base rate, epoch ceiling, partner share, and the tier thresholds and multipliers — take effect — after they are queued, and a queued change can be canceled before it applies.

Other settings — the partner cap, the fee-tier whitelist, the entry minimum under the PSRE-native version, the staker-pool split, and pausing — are set by the Founder Safe and take effect immediately, without a timelock.

Upgrades to the RewardEngine take effect — after they are scheduled. Under the PSRE-native version, replacing the partner-vault factory is a second step with its own, subsequent 7-day delay, so a full migration passes two timelocks in sequence. The PSRE token contract itself is not upgradeable.

The 21 million cap and the 12.6 million reserve are constants in the contracts.

Changes are proposed and executed by the Founder Safe under the timelocks above. No token vote exists today and there is no DAO.
